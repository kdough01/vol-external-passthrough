--- what to look for ---
  chunk dims : any key matching /chunk/. If present, set it to cover the
               whole (sub)volume so SPERR emits ONE internal chunk.
  error mode : the key selecting PWE vs PSNR vs fixed-rate, and whether
               pressio:abs is among the settable options at all.
  If NO chunk key exists, SPERR uses its 256^3 default and a byte prefix
  of the stream is not independently decodable -- go to Plan B (the
  256^3 crop) in bench_config_sperr.md.
